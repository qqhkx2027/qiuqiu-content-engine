**1.手写字体生成器**

**![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_jpg/PibuibB1Eez6AEtFaoJz8a6utf8vAojByNyicA89jF6WX1RcgFcwZsib4S3WnISib6bWlQsYfnHtJDw1Z8CKZZcQd3Q/640?wx_fmt=jpeg)**

可这个，**不仅里面自带多种字体**，你还能选择，去**模拟出**你的真实字体。

  

![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_jpg/PibuibB1Eez6AEtFaoJz8a6utf8vAojByNOQVyF8aQhwyPRRicPwHQcOVOxWINpv8EEX4WsQwSJzQQsqpfsjkynHg/640?wx_fmt=jpeg)

![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/PibuibB1Eez6AEtFaoJz8a6utf8vAojByN2xKxxmWs1mqgpHJHibn4cWib9j66BtywVhbUbia7NKfd0V2FPCJwJOTRQ/640?wx_fmt=png)

  

![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/PibuibB1Eez6AEtFaoJz8a6utf8vAojByNDNJXiajcMTGlYlibQqtTDjZWRoqHknCSo7yict4rTLxN8LI3Rb8G5YJ9Q/640?wx_fmt=png)

  

我真的，感叹科技的伟大！
